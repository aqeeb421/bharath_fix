  • A non-refundable inspection/visiting fee (e.g. ₹199) covers the technician's doorstep visit and diagnosis.

  • Product sales are fulfilled with manufacturer warranty and free or optional doorstep installation as indicated at checkout.

5. OTP Security Validation

To ensure customer safety and work accuracy, all service jobs require a 4-digit Start OTP (given to technician upon arrival) and a Completion OTP (given upon completion). Do not share OTPs before the technician arrives or before inspecting finished work.

Bharath Fix provides a 30-day service warranty on repair work executed by our technicians. Manufacturer warranties apply directly to brand-new appliance purchases as per manufacturer terms.
