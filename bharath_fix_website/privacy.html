Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: August 25, 2026

Welcome to Bharath Fix Services ("Bharath Fix", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to safeguarding your personal data and ensuring transparency regarding how we collect, process, and protect your information when you use our mobile applications (Customer App & Technician App) and our website at https://bharathfix.netlify.app/.

1. Information We Collect

To provide reliable doorstep home repair services and appliance retail delivery, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account & Contact Information: Full name, phone number, email address, and service delivery addresses.
  • Location Data (Precise & Coarse): We collect your precise GPS location and address details to assign nearest certified technicians, calculate delivery distances, and navigate technicians to your doorstep.
  • Device & Identifiers: Device model, OS version, Firebase FCM Tokens, and IP address for sending real-time push notifications and security authentication.
  • Media & Camera Access: Optional access to your device camera or gallery when you choose to upload photos of broken appliances to obtain accurate repair cost estimates.
  • Payment & Transaction Data: Transaction reference IDs, in-app service credit balance, and payment mode (UPI, Cards, Netbanking, COD). Note: We do NOT store credit card CVV numbers or banking passwords. All online transactions are securely handled by certified payment gateways (Razorpay).

2. Non-Financial Services Declaration

Bharath Fix is exclusively an on-demand home maintenance and appliance repair service marketplace. We do NOT provide banking services, loans, financial credit instruments, cryptocurrency wallets, money transfer, or regulated financial products. In-app service credits are solely internal reward/refund vouchers used to adjust service bookings.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule, dispatch, and fulfill doorstep appliance repair and installation bookings.
  • To process bookings and issue automatic in-app service credit adjustments for cancelled service orders.
  • To generate 4-digit security OTPs for job start and completion verification.
  • To send booking confirmation SMS, WhatsApp alerts, and FCM push notifications.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or fraudulent activities.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only with essential service providers necessary for app operations:

  • Google Firebase: Used for database management (Firestore), authentication, and push notifications.
  • Razorpay Software Pvt Ltd: Used for PCI-DSS compliant payment processing.
  • Assigned Technicians: Assigned technicians receive your name, phone number, and service address solely for completing your booked service request.

4. Data Retention & Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS / SSL) and Firestore security rules to protect your personal information. We retain your booking history and transaction data only for as long as required to comply with financial and legal obligations.

5. Account & Data Deletion Rights

Requesting Data Deletion: You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

To submit an account deletion request, please email us at bharathfixservice@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" along with your registered mobile phone number. Our team will verify and remove your account and personal records within 7 business days.
